Can You Spot These for Buying — or Only Selling?

Short answer

Both — but the edge is asymmetric. The same red flags that warn you to exit are the same signals that can help you ride the pump early. Shorting is the hardest and most dangerous play. Holding through the top is how retail gets destroyed.

Strategy Edge Risk Verdict
Buy early pump High reward if timed Insiders can dump anytime; you're exit liquidity Possible — strict rules only
Hold through top None -90% in 48h is normal Never
Exit when deposits hit CEX High — on-chain is early warning May leave some upside on table Best risk/reward
Short the top Theoretical Insiders squeeze shorts with thin float ZachXBT discourages
Buy the "falling knife" Low 80M+ tokens may still be held by insiders Avoid (LAB, SIREN post-crash)

Why VELVET & SIREN felt like "good opportunities"

You caught the pump phase — the part insiders build on purpose. A thin float + narrative (SpaceX pre-IPO for VELVET, AI agent for SIREN) + futures listing = violent upside. That is tradeable if you treat it as a momentum scalp, not an investment.

Pump traders win when
  • Enter early (before CEX deposit warnings)
  • Position size is small (1–3% of portfolio)
  • Take profit in tiers (25% / 50% / 75%)
  • Hard stop below structure
  • Exit fully on team wallet → exchange flows
Pump traders lose when
  • They "believe the narrative"
  • They add on the way up near ATH
  • They ignore Bubblemaps / ZachXBT warnings
  • They short into a squeeze
  • They catch the falling knife after crash

The Repeatable Playbook

Investigator ZachXBT tied LAB, RAVE, RIVER, SIREN, MYX, SKYAI, and EDGE to the same structure — an alleged market maker operating primarily through Bitget, with Binance and Gate as secondary channels.

Launch (AI/DeFi narrative, BNB Chain) ↓ Keep 90–95% supply in insider wallets / OTC / treasury ↓ List on Bitget → Gate → Binance Alpha → Binance Perps ↓ Deposit millions of tokens to CEX addresses (often dormant weeks) ↓ Activate market maker → vertical pump → retail FOMO ↓ Futures OI explodes → liquidations fuel the move ↓ Insiders distribute via OTC (60–80% discount) + open market sells ↓ -85% to -97% in days → retail holds the bag

Five phases — and where you can act

1
Stealth accumulation — Insiders load wallets, OTC deals at steep discounts. Hard to see
2
CEX listing + dormant deposits — Tokens sit on Bitget/Gate deposit addresses. Watch Arkham, Nansen
3
Pump activation — Price goes vertical; volume/OI ratio spikes. Buy zone (small size)
4
Distribution — Team wallets move to exchanges and sell. Exit immediately
5
Collapse — Liquidity gone; bleeding continues. Do not buy dip

Two manipulation models (Odaily analysis)

Model A — Short Squeeze (MYX)

Accumulate → lure shorts → liquidate them → pump → insiders short the top and distribute.

Model B — Pure Pump & Dump (COAI, LAB)

<20% circulating at launch → immediate vertical move → no pause → off-exchange OTC extraction.

Spotting Checklist

Run this on any token before you buy — and again daily if you're in a position.

Supply & Tokenomics

  • !Float < 20% of total supply circulating
  • !FDV / Market Cap > 5× — massive future overhang
  • !Conflicting supply data across CoinGecko, CMC, and project docs
  • !One cluster holds 50–95% — check Bubblemaps, Arkham
  • ?Unlock > 10% of float in a single day — exit or avoid entry
  • ?OTC menus on Telegram/WhatsApp at 60–80% discount (LAB pattern)

On-Chain Signals

  • !Large CEX deposits (Bitget, Gate, Binance) — especially if dormant then active
  • !Fresh wallet clusters receiving millions of tokens before a pump
  • !Consolidation to 10+ new addresses days before price spike (LAB: 100M to 10 wallets)
  • ?Same wallets across tokens — SIREN cluster linked to DWF-affiliated addresses
  • ✓Dormant insider wallets wake up = distribution starting — your exit signal

Market Structure

  • !Volume ≈ Market Cap in 24h — entire float changing hands (panic or distribution)
  • !Volume / Open Interest > 20× on perps — liquidation-driven pump
  • ?Binance Alpha → Perps pipeline — common 2026 pattern
  • ?AI / pre-IPO / agent narrative with no real user growth
  • !Founders' prior project rugged — LAB founders ran Eesee (-93%)
The 60-second screen
  1. Bubblemaps → any cluster > 30%? Stop.
  2. tokenunlocks.app → cliff within 2 weeks? Stop or scalp only.
  3. FDV/MC ratio → above 5? Extreme caution.
  4. Search "[TOKEN] ZachXBT" on X → existing investigation? Strong avoid for holds.
  5. Arkham → exchange inflows last 48h? If long, take profit now.

Trading Rules — Personal Playbook

Entry rules
  • Max 1–3% portfolio per low-float token
  • Only enter Phase 2–3 (post-listing, pre-distribution)
  • Narrative must be fresh (not "already played" like SpaceX)
  • No entry if ZachXBT investigation already live
  • No entry within 2 weeks of major unlock
  • Prefer tokens where you can define risk (clear support level)
Exit rules (non-negotiable)
  • Take 50% profit at +50% gain minimum
  • Full exit on any team → CEX transfer report
  • Full exit if Bubblemaps flags >40% cluster
  • Full exit if 24h volume > 50% of mcap
  • Never average down after a -30% dump
  • Never hold low-float tokens overnight unhedged near ATH
SIREN-style "still falling" — what to watch

If SIREN (or any post-crash token) is still bleeding:

  • Insider wallets may still hold tens of millions of tokens
  • Dead-cat bounces are 10–30% — good for scalps only, not reversals
  • Wait for supply to actually disperse (cluster < 20%) before any "investment" thesis
  • Perp funding negative + rising OI = someone betting on another squeeze — stay cautious
Mental model

"You're not investing in a project. You're renting momentum from insiders — and they always take the car back."
